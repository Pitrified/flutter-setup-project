## LLM principles & patterns

in `llm-core` we have a whole set of tools for building LLM-powered things, but all in python with langchain.
spin up a subagent to deep dive into the relevant parts of `llm-core` and extract the principles and patterns that make sense for us, in our context, and in our tech stack (Flutter + Dart).
we do not need to abstract it all in some `llm-core-dart`, but we can borrow some principles if they make sense in our context.
if something is too cumbersome to replicate, we can consider other options.
building a layer to connect to `llm-core` is completely out of scope, `llm-core` is only for reference.
