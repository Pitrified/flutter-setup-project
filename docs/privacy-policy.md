# Privacy Policy - fala

**Last updated:** 2024

## Overview

fala is an offline language-learning app that runs entirely on your device.

## Data collection

fala does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external
servers. Specifically:

- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising
- No third-party services
- No network requests after the initial model download
- No account creation required

## Local data

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

- **Conversation history**: Your practice conversations are saved on-device
  for your reference. This data never leaves your device.
- **Language model**: A downloaded AI model file stored in app-private storage.
- **App preferences**: Basic settings stored locally.

## Model download

On first launch, the app downloads a language model file from our servers.
This is the only network request the app makes. No personal information
is sent during this download.

## Data deletion

All local data can be deleted by:
- Clearing app data in Android Settings
- Uninstalling the app

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
Since no data is collected from any user, this is not applicable.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be noted in app updates.
